Locations & units

Locations

Locations tell you where food is stored, such as Pantry, Fridge or Freezer. Dates come from each packet or the product’s usual shelf life.

Units

Pack sizes

Set pack sizes on each product: count it in pieces, choose pack under “Bought as”, then enter how many pieces are in one pack. Buying 2 packs of 6 adds 12 pieces.

Automatic conversions

Weight and volume convert automatically, for example kg to g and l to ml. Fluid ounces use US volume. These rules do not turn packs into pieces.

Custom conversions

For general rules that apply to every product. Product pack sizes belong on the product.
